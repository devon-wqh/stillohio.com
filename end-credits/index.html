About Us

End Credits is a magazine about film and the arts, written for the people who stay in their seats until the house lights come up.

We publish profiles, interviews and criticism, and keep a running list of screenings and releases worth leaving the house (or staying in) for. The name is the brief: we’re interested in everyone on the roll, not just the names above the title.
